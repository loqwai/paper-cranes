# lattice-bead/4.frag — the legible bead

**Forked from** `3.frag` on **2026-09-04**, overnight, while the user slept.
This is the iteration where the project's actual acceptance test finally passes:
**a stranger, at a distance, in a dark room, can name their own bead.**

## The problem, as measured

Recognition had failed at every framing and every seed pitch, and this session finally
established *why* rather than guessing:

- All 11 mon were near-indistinguishable at playing framing. The dominant shapes in frame are
  produced by the **lattice fold**, not the motif — 76.1% common effect, only **7.9pt**
  between-motif spread (`kb/reference/lattice-bead-h7-motif-ranking.md`).
- `knob_169` swept 0.10 → 0.55 against `knob_168` 0.55/0.9/1.0 gave **15 near-identical tiles**.
- `navZoom` moved the framing but made a bead legible at **no** setting.

The cause is **figure/ground**. 3.frag deliberately let the lattice texture survive *inside* the
motif — its own comment calls that a feature. But it means interior and exterior carry the same
contrast, so the eye has no silhouette to lock onto and the shape never resolves.

## The fix: LEGIBLE

One hand lever, `knob_180` or `?legible=0..1`, default **0** (which reproduces 3.frag exactly):

1. **Interior flatten** — the inside collapses toward the bead's own ink, making the silhouette
   the dominant edge in frame. *This is the move that actually buys recognition.*
2. **Deeper recede** — the ground between beads drops from 3.frag's 0.25–0.50 to 0.58–0.82.
3. **Thicker contour** — the rim widens from a 4× hairline to a 16× drawn line that survives
   being downscaled to a phone or thrown across a room.

Every term is `cov`/`rim`-masked and spatially structured — **no global multiplier**, so none of
it can strobe the frame. LEGIBLE is a **hand knob**, so no geometry moves with the music. (2) is
a ratchet, so (1) and (3) are its **counter-ratchet in the same edit**: a deeper recede takes
light out of most of the frame and the figure pays it back.

## Size is a prerequisite, and it is a WINDOW

The lever alone moved mean luminance only 103.4 → 106.6, because at the shipped `knob_169=0.28`
a cell is `exp2(mix(-6,2,0.28))` = **0.074** in uv — tens of beads across the frame, each a few
pixels wide. Nothing makes a shape that small nameable.

But bigger is **not** monotonically better. Past `knob_169 = 0.75` the cell grows so large the
camera sits *inside* a single bead and the silhouette disappears entirely. It is a window:

| knob_169 | cell pitch (uv) | reads as |
|---|---|---|
| 0.28 (shipped) | 0.074 | a fine texture — no shape |
| **0.55 – 0.65** | **0.33 – 0.57** | **the bead, with dark ground around it** |
| 0.75 | 1.00 | edge of usable |
| 0.85 – 0.95 | 1.74 – 3.03 | inside one bead — no silhouette |

## The recognition recipe

```
knob_169 = 0.60   navZoom = 0.14   legible = 1   knob_168 = 1.0
```

At this setting **all eleven mon are individually nameable**: suhama's three mounds, ogi's fan,
kikyo's bellflower, ume's five petals, katabami's hearts, tomoe's comma-and-tail, mokko's melon,
kikko's hexagon, kiku's chrysanthemum, matsukawa's lozenge, hakkaku's eight-pointed star.
Verified in `journals/lab/shots/bigbead-mon.png`, and **confirmed live** on the rig with audio
running and `autofly` moving the camera.

Each mon ships as a preset in the `.frag`, so every bead is one tap from the list page.

## A bug this caught in itself

LEGIBLE was first written with the house `LVK(k, baked, map)` convention — "0 means unset, use
the baked default". That convention **cannot express zero**, so `?legible=0` silently became 0.55
and the two rendered bit-identically (mean luminance 103.37 for both). Caught by the sweep, not
by reading. It is now `clamp(max(knob_180, legible), 0.0, 1.0)`, which has no unset state, so 0
is reachable and the "legible=0 reproduces 3.frag" claim is actually true.

## Open

- With `autofly` on, the framing drifts through zoom levels, so the bead alternates between
  reading as figure (glowing) and as ground (dark). Both are legible, but the "wall of beads"
  read is best held near the sweep framing. Whether autofly should be range-limited when
  `legible > 0` is a judgement call left for the user.
- The jam drawer registers `paletteShift` with `.min=0 .max=1`, but the good values run to 1.35.
  Same trap as `onset_refractory_ms`. Touching that slider loses the palette.
