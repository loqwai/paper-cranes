# lattice-vj/5 — "evolve, breathe, fly"

**Forked from `4.frag` on 2026-08-19**, live, mid-set (tab audio, room `r017686ce73`), at the end of
the iters 143–149b run. This is the first state where all three of the user's long-running complaints
were resolved *at once* and confirmed live: no bouncing, no global brightness wash, and crisp lines.

`4.frag` stays as the working scratch target; `5.frag` is the snapshot of the good state.

## What this iteration is

The synthesis of the 138→149 arc. Three channels, three different rules:

| Channel | Rule | What carries it here |
|---|---|---|
| **Spatial frame** | never moves with audio or oscillators | fold ratio `gScale`, every `theta` term, per-level parity |
| **Structure inside a cell** | free to breathe/morph | `gHexR` (mids/wub), `gCross` (bass), `gBorder` (crest), `gDepthFocus` (treble, small) |
| **Light** | takes the audio, as RELIEF not gain | line-vs-fill contrast, per-depth band lighting |
| **Colour** | slowest music only | key median, set clock, permanent drop mutations |

### The two rules that took five user complaints to find

1. **Audio modulates RATE / SHAPE / AMPLITUDE — never an ANGLE.** Anything added to an angle that can
   *fall* will unwind, and unwinding is the "bouncing". Audit every term inside `rot2()`/`theta`,
   not just the parameter assignments — that's why iter 142 looked clean and still bounced.
2. **The veto is about SPACE, not oscillation.** (User, iter 144: "oscillation _can_ be ok - just not
   large moving pieces that disrupt the sense of space. The fractal structures can breathe and
   morph.") Modulating the fold ratio moves every mirror seam at every level and the error compounds
   as `scale^i` — that is what destroys the sense of space. Cell-interior modulation doesn't.


**Controller order matters**: `lattice-nav` then `flyby` — repeated `?controller=` params are pipeline
stages and the last one wins on a key clash, so `flyby` must come after `lattice-nav` to compose its
travel offset on top of finger nav rather than being overwritten by it.

`navZoom=0.45` is deliberate: at zoom 1.0 the lattice sits close enough that the soft half of the line
profile spans many pixels and reads soft. ~0.45 is the tuned framing. (This only works as of the
2026-08-19 `lattice-nav` fix — before it, URL nav params were silently ignored.)

## Companion controller

`controllers/flyby.js` (new this iteration). Cycle: cruise 26 s → ease wide 7 s → travel 13 s →
ease in 7 s, departing early on a drop once it has held the view a while. Travel happens **only while
wide** — panning at full magnification is what read as "scrolling" in the vj2 iter-2 veto. Every leg
is a one-way smoothstep; rates are audio-free so a transient can never lurch the camera. It keeps its
accumulated offset, so each hop departs from where the last ended and the journey never revisits.

## Known issues carried into 5

- `paletteShift` accumulates **far faster than intended**: it is meant to be a rare permanent mutation
  on big drops, but the detector (`ez > 1.4 || hit > 0.85`, 2 s cooldown) fired ~330 times in ~20 min
  (≈ every 3.6 s), taking paletteShift from 0 to **90.3**. It wraps, so it is not visibly broken, but
  it is not the "rare permanent mutation on a big drop" the design intends. Tighten the threshold or
  lengthen the cooldown.
- `hueDriftPerMin` spikes (~0.4–0.7) during drops from the deliberate palette glide; ~0 between them.
  Judge it only on drop-free clean-gate windows.
