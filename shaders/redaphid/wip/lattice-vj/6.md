# lattice-vj/6 — "the explore fork"

**Forked from `5.frag` on 2026-08-19.** `5.frag` remains the good, live-verified snapshot
("evolve, breathe, fly"). `6.frag` is identical to it *by default* and exists for one job:

> Every constant in the fractal that was tuned by hand over 150 iterations and has never been
> reachable live is now a fader — so you can fly it **with the music off** and find out what it
> actually does, before deciding it deserves an audio feature.

## The one rule: 0.5 is the tuned value

Every EXPLORE fader is **centred**. `knob = 0.5` reproduces `5.frag`'s constant exactly, so:

- a freshly reset EXPLORE bank is a byte-identical look to `5.frag`
- distance from centre reads directly as *"how far off the tuned number am I"*
- no phone on the bank → knobs read 0 → the `EXA_ON`/`EXB_ON` gate zeroes the whole bank
  (without that gate an absent phone would read as **every fader slammed down**)

Two macros carry it:

```glsl
#define EXA(k, amt) ((k - 0.5) * (amt) * EXA_ON)   // knob_141–150, geometry
#define EXB(k, amt) ((k - 0.5) * (amt) * EXB_ON)   // knob_151–160, colour field
```

## EXPLORE A — geometry (knob_141–150, vjpad bank "EXPLORE A")

| Pad | Knob | Name | Constant it replaces | What moving it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FOLD | 141 | TWIST STEP | `PI * 0.125` per-level fold rotation | the master symmetry control — re-tiles the whole plane. Also feeds the zoom-wrap compensation (shared as `gThetaStep`, or the octave seam snaps). |
| FOLD | 142 | TWIST FALL | `0.4 + i * 0.05` spin weighting | how much harder deep levels spin than shallow. Centre-down = the stack turns as one rigid piece. |
| CELL | 143 | INTERLEAVE | iter150's `lvlParity * 0.16` | pushes even/odd levels to opposite radii — one lattice becomes two interleaved ones. **This is the manual version of the `spectralSkewMedian` term**: fly it dry to see what that feature is really doing. |
| CELL | 144 | RING GAP | the `- 0.1` in `delt1` | how hollow a cell is, independent of its radius. |
| SPIN | 145 | LEVEL SKEW | `(seed3-0.5) * i * 0.8` | per-level twist depth. 0 = every device sees the same lattice. |
| SPIN | 146 | SPIN RATE | `bTime * 0.18` | the monotonic fold rotation. **Clamped at 0** — a fader can stall the spin, never reverse it (standing directive: geometry always goes forward). |
| ZOOM | 147 | ZOOM RATE | `bTime * 0.016` | how fast the perpetual self-similar zoom eats one octave (~60 s at centre). `max()`-clamped one-way. |
| ZOOM | 148 | OCTAVE | `pow(2.0, zoomP)` base | what magnification counts as one wrap. The seam only vanishes when this **matches the fold ratio** (`gScale`, K131) — mismatching is a legitimate look, it just visibly re-snaps each cycle. |
| SURFACE | 149 | FILL | `gFill = 0.06` | cells solid vs hollow outlines. |
| SURFACE | 150 | WARP | `0.03` terrain warp | 0 = a perfectly rigid lattice; high = the plane sags between landmarks. |

## EXPLORE B — colour field (knob_151–160, vjpad bank "EXPLORE B")

| Pad | Knob | Name | Constant | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SWIRL | 151 | ARMS | `atan(p.y,p.x) * 2.0` | arm count of the within-cell colour swirl |
| SWIRL | 152 | RADIAL | `length(p) * 3.0` | radial frequency of that swirl |
| FIELD | 153 | DEPTH TINT | `ld * 0.55` | how much recursion **depth** colours the field |
| FIELD | 154 | SWIRL MIX | `swirl * 0.45` | how much the **swirl** does. The 153/154 balance is what makes colour read as depth vs as flow. |
| HUE | 155 | REGION | `regionHue()` amplitude | 0 = one colour everywhere; high = every screen you pan to is a new colourway |
| HUE | 156 | LIGHT BASE | `L = 0.33 + …` | palette baseline lightness (0.33 took five iterations of "less washed out") |
| COLOUR | 157 | CHROMA | `C = 0.075 + …` | saturation. 0 = greyscale — genuinely the best way to *read* the geometry. |
| COLOUR | 158 | ACCENT | `wave * 0.6` | strength of the travelling pulse accent |
| TONE | 159 | BG FLOOR | `bg * 0.30` | how lit the field between cells is; low = the lattice floats in the dark |
| TONE | 160 | GAIN | `col *= 1.06` | global exposure, **clamped 0.25–1.9** so a slammed fader can't white the frame out |

## Standing directives respected

The iters 138–150 vetoes still hold and are enforced in the wiring, not left to discipline:

- **SPIN RATE and ZOOM RATE are `max(0.0, …)`** — a fader can stall geometry but never run it
  backward. "Always going forward" (iter 118) survives a hand on the pad.
- **GAIN is clamped** — directive #1 is no global brightness wash, so the manual exposure fader
  can't reintroduce it.
- TWIST STEP is shared between `fractal()` and the zoom-wrap compensation via `gThetaStep`, so
  flying it doesn't tear open the ~60 s octave seam.

## Running it (no music)

```
http://localhost:6969/?shader=redaphid/wip/lattice-vj/6&remote=display&controller=lattice-nav&noaudio=true&knob_1=0.627&knob_131=0.162&knob_132=0.483&knob_133=0.59&knob_134=0.507&knob_135=0.876&knob_136=0.57&knob_137=0.485&knob_138=0.505&knob_139=0.216&knob_140=0.537&navZoom=0.45&paletteShift=0.28
```

Pad: `http://localhost:6969/vjpad.html` → pick **EXPLORE A** or **EXPLORE B**.

`noaudio=true` and no `wavelet=true` means `quietGate` is 0, so every audio term drops out and the
structure holds still — which is the point: a constant you change is the *only* thing moving.
No `flyby` controller either; the camera should not wander while you are auditing a constant.
`lattice-nav` stays on for drag-pan / pinch-zoom.
