# lattice-vj/8 — "the structure snapshot"

**Forked from `7.frag` on 2026-08-20**, at the end of the live-mic `/vibej2` run. `7.frag` was the
run's scratch target; `8.frag` is the keeper — same code, plus the session's earned state baked
into the preset header.

## Origin

- **Music:** live mic, room audio (no tab share, no audio file).
- **Session:** `/vibej2` responsive loop, ~5 hours wall clock, 7 structural beats.
- **Brief that shaped it** (user, at run start): *"concentrate on the structural elements of the
  fractal itself — I want to see the various shapes of the visuals 'dancing' — maybe there's a way
  to algorithmically add complexity?"*

## What this iteration is

Everything here obeys the channel hierarchy (geometry EVOLVES only; light takes the audio; colour
follows the slowest music). The "dance" is therefore never a per-beat wiggle — it is one-way steps,
monotonic rates, and navigation:

| Beat | Change | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| b1 | **Symmetry plateau** — each drop eases `gThetaStep` to a new hashed value (±π·0.08), so the whole plane re-tiles and *stays*. Section 0 pinned to the tuned π/8. | one-way plateau |
| b2 | **Spin follows the low end** — fold rotation rate = reduced constant + `flowPhase·0.05` (bass-paced, monotonic, silence-frozen). | rate-not-angle |
| b4 | **Spatial permutations** — `gThetaStep`, `gInterleave`, `gTwistFall`, `gScale` are smooth incommensurate functions of world x/y. Panning continuously re-tiles the lattice; stand still and it stands still. **The PATH ribbon and landmark towers were removed** (user request) — navigation itself is now the reward. | user-driven |
| b5/b6 | **Zoom × loudness** — sustained energy slows the perpetual self-similar dive, via `(bTime − evoPhase·25)·rate`. LEARNED from a confirmed fader gesture (K147 vs `energyLong`, r=−0.75, t=4.96). | rate-not-angle |
| b7 | **Complexity ratchet** — `gComplex = 1 − exp(−evoPhase·0.33)`; the recursion-level window dissolves (`gLevelOpen`, capped 0.45) and the two sub-lattices separate further. The lattice gains detail across a set and never gives it back. | monotonic set clock |

## Caveat carried forward from 7

The zoom×loudness coefficient (`evoPhase * 25.0`) was calibrated to a **quiet room mic**. At real
venue volume `evoPhase` advances faster and the term can over-slow or reverse the zoom — drop it
back toward 10, or have the controller emit a normalized sustained-energy rate instead. The
in-source comment says so at the call site.
