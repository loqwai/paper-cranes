## The two learned mappings

Both came from LEARN presses (the pad button that says "this gesture was deliberate — analyse it"),
and both cleared the corroboration bar: *more than one fader, independently, pointing at the same
feature.*

### CHURN — spectral flux → line weight + rim flare (b10)

| Fader | Feature | r | lag | t | n_eff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K147 ZOOM RATE | spectralFluxNormalized | +0.57 | 0 s | 5.22 | 58.8 |
| K150 WARP | spectralFluxNormalized | +0.57 | 2 s | 4.77 | 49.3 |
| K149 FILL | spectralFluxNormalized | −0.45 | 2 s | 3.49 | 50.8 |

```glsl
#define CHURN_RAW (0.70 * spectralFluxNormalized + 0.55 * max(0.0, spectralFluxZScore))
#define CHURN (clamp((CHURN_RAW - 0.10) * 4.0, 0.0, 1.0) * quietGate)
```

A **composite of two views of one feature**: the normalized level says where in the range we are,
the one-sided z-score adds the burst. Drives `gBorder` (+0.035) and the rim flare (+0.80): churn
thickens and lights the lattice; a clean passage lets it go fine and precise.

### WUB — wobble depth → travelling accent + specular glint (b11)

| Fader | Feature | r | lag | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K106 (COLOUR pad Y) | wubDepth | +0.521 | 0 s | 5.70 |
| K105 (COLOUR pad X) | wubDepth | +0.448 | 0 s | 5.00 |

#define WUB (clamp((wubDepth - 0.18) * 1.25, 0.0, 1.0) * quietGate)

Drives the travelling accent (+0.9) and the specular glint (+0.55): a wobbly bassline surges the

## The rules these mappings taught (the expensive part)

1. **Range-fit every feature to its OWN measured span.** CHURN raw only spanned 0.111–0.319 live and
   needed a ×4 expansion to read at all; wubDepth already spanned 0.184–1.0 and needed only a floor
   trim. A coefficient carried over from another signal produces either an invisible mapping or a
   slammed one. Measuring takes two minutes.
2. **Check the target has headroom in the CURRENT preset.** The first wiring put churn on FILL —
   the sign the hand actually traced — but the user's own K149 sat at 0, so the subtractive term had
   nothing left to remove and only pushed `gFill` negative, unlighting every cell interior
   (lumMin 0.051, three too-dark alerts). Line weight always has headroom; FILL did not.
3. **A subtractive audio term needs an OUTER clamp**, or it walks past the floor its manual control
   already sits on.
4. **Adding structure or removing lit area needs a counter-ratchet in the same edit** — here the rim
   flare and a gap-light term that lifts field lightness only when cells are hollow.
