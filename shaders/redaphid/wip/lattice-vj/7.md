## How "dancing" is legal here

The standing channel hierarchy (HANDOFF, iters 138–150) forbids audio and oscillators on fold
geometry. So structural dance uses the two channels that ARE allowed:

1. **Section plateaus** — one-way ~4 s eased steps on detected drops (`sectionMode`/`sectionMix`).
   The dance move happens at musical boundaries and *stays*.
2. **Rate modulation of monotonic motion** — audio may speed/stall the always-forward spin/zoom,
   never reverse or wiggle it.
3. **Algorithmic complexity** — structure parameters grow richer as a monotonic function of the
   set clock (`evoPhase`), so the lattice *earns* complexity over the night.
