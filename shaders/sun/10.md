# sun/10: fractal day sky with lattice

Forked 2026-10-09, about 21:58 MST, during the live party set. It is a copy of the shader that was compiled and running on the wall at that moment (`claude/wip/black-sun/sun-1-wip`).

## Origin

`sun-1-wip` with all of the evening's live edits:

- **NO-SHIVER-2/3/4.** The audio no longer pushes geometry. The kick swells only the ball, not the disc.
- **LIMB-1b.** The sun is a burning sphere with a pale amber core and a darker red rim.
- **COMPANION-LOOK-1.** The companion sun is drawn in the same way as the main sun.
- **GLOW-PULSE-1.** The filament brightness follows smoothed bass and mids.
- **COLLAPSE-LIFT-1b.** COLLAPSE is about 25% brighter.
- **NO-FLASH-1.** Brightness swells instead of blinking.
- **SCENES-1f / SCENES-2b.** The ORBIT and ECLIPSE scene cycle. NO-SPACE gates it off in this fork.
- **SKY-RETURN-1.** The original patchwork day sky is back.
- **FRACTAL-SKY-1 / FRACTAL-CALM-1b.** The sky is self-similar with no space looks. It zooms one octave in about 5 minutes and rotates slowly about the centre, with no drift.
- **LATTICE-MIX-1.** The `redaphid/lattice-interactive/3` pattern is blended into the sky at 0.6.
- **EVO-WIRE-1.** The slow controller mood levels drive octave emphasis, the lattice palette walk and the lattice amount.

The runtime ramps are baked in as constants, so the fork shows this look on a fresh load: `sky_return = 1.0`, `no_space = 1.0`, `lattice_mix = 0.6`.

## Controller

`controllers/sun-10.js` is a frozen snapshot of `controllers/black-sun.js`, with the evo, mood and drop/build/shift outputs. Its state global is renamed to `window.__sun10State` so it can't collide with the live controller.
