# sun/12

Frozen fork of `claude/wip/black-sun/sun-1-wip`, taken from the live wall on 2026-10-09, about 22:45 MST, during the show.
The controller is snapshotted as `controllers/sun-12.js`, a copy of `black-sun.js`.

## Why this fork

The painterly pass: brush-stroke tendrils (PAINT-2), broken OKLCH colour, canvas grain and glazes.
The ECLIPSE black moon is removed (NO-ECLIPSE-1). K10 is now PAINT, no longer FADE.
The tendril params are driven by unused audio features through `#define` lines (TENDRIL-AUDIO-1), with the knob versions on commented-out lines.
