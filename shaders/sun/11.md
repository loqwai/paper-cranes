# sun/11

Frozen fork of the live wall (`claude/wip/black-sun/sun-1-wip`), taken 2026-10-09 ~22:20 MST during the show.

## Origin

The sun-1-wip state at fork time:

- Sun cast (SUN-CAST-1b): blue-white dwarf, red giant, pulsar, binary pair. The cast ramp replays from `bs_time` 0 on a fresh load.
- PERF-CAST-1: the cast skips the plasma surface past each limb, so the frame stays at 60 fps.
- ZOOM-K4-1: K4 flies into the sky fractal and lattice and scales the sun.
- Fractal day sky (SKY-RETURN, FRACTAL-SKY, FRACTAL-CALM) with the lattice-interactive/3 lattice blended in (LATTICE-MIX). Space looks are off.
- Earlier passes: NO-SHIVER-2/3/4, LIMB-1b, GLOW-PULSE-1, COMPANION-LOOK-1, COLLAPSE-LIFT-1b, SCENES-2b, NO-FLASH-1, EVO-WIRE-1.

LATTICE-FEAT-1 (the `bs_f*` feature bank) was not live yet, so this fork does not read any `bs_f*` uniforms, and the controller snapshot does not produce them.
