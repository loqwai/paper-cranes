## Controller

`controllers/sun-16.js` is a frozen copy of `black-sun.js` from the moment of the fork. It keeps its
state on `window.__blackSunState`, the same global the live controller uses. Load it in a fresh
page, and never hot-swap it into the live page. A panic hot-swap of `sun/16` changes only the
shader, so the live controller keeps feeding it `bs_*`.

## Cold start

In `sun-1-show` the fade-ins and phase anchors are baked against the live page's `bs_time`, so a
reload shows the pre-giant look until they are re-baked (see RELOAD-REBAKE in
`shaders/claude/wip/black-sun/black-sun.md`). This fork bakes them for a cold start instead. Every
`FADE_T*` except `FADE_T2` is `-1000.0`, so every layer is fully in at `bs_time` 0. `BUD0`–`BUD2`,
`FLX2`, `C_FLUX0`, `FLUX0` and `FLOW0` are `0.0`, so the forward clocks run from boot. `FADE_T2`,
`GAL_T2` and `GAL_T8` are unchanged; with `FADE_T8` in the past, the sky warp holds its glided-to-a-stop
value. The lines are marked `COLD-START`. The look appears at once on a fresh boot or a panic swap,
with no 60–75 s fade-in.
