# cozy-neon-night — Ambient Cyberpunk

## Design Intent
A warm, soothing cyberpunk aesthetic designed for ambient room lighting. Concentric neon rings pulse gently in warm orange, coral, and teal against a very dark background. Perfect for creating a cozy, futuristic bedtime vibe while driving nanoleaf lights via screen scraping.

## Visual Features
- **Warm orange/coral rings**: Primary visual element, pulsing gently
- **Warm purple accents**: Secondary layer for depth
- **Teal/cyan undertones**: Cyberpunk cold touch balanced by warmth
- **Radial breathing**: Slow center glow that grows and shrinks
- **Dark background**: Deep navy/black for coziness and to avoid harsh brightness
- **Soft vignette falloff**: Edges fade to black naturally
- **Gentle bloom**: Subtle glow effect for ambient warmth

## Audio Mapping
Non-audio-reactive by design. Use with `?noaudio=true` for pure ambient mode.

## Performance
- Lightweight raymarching
- Mobile-friendly
- No audio processing overhead
- Smooth 60fps on all devices

## Nanoleaf Recommendations
- Position display device 1-2 feet from nanoleaf array
- Use in dim room for best color accuracy
- The warm orange and teal create a balanced visual field for screen scraping
