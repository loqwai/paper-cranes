## Design hypotheses for v(next)
- **Whole-frame saturation is meaningless on a mostly-black frame** (iter27). `sat` fell 0.75→0.54 across three ticks purely because `dark` rose 0.70→0.79; `satOfLitPixels` read 0.987. Any whole-frame mean (sat, hue concentration) needs a lit-pixel mask before it can be compared across ticks where the dark fraction moved.
- **Under a hard two-tone selector, the hue coordinate's amplitude decides WHETHER a driver is visible at all** (iter25). `wave*0.10` had been live since 74ef410 and did nothing once the 0.44–0.56 lock landed — it never crossed the band; 0.18 does. After a palette lock, re-read every hue driver's amplitude against the selector width, not just its rate (iter21 was the rate half of this rule).
- **Count the consumers of a feature before wiring another** (iter23). Energy already fed build and (via the arc) gain; adding width made a loud passage widen + densify + brighten together — dark dropped 0.16 in one window. Each feature should have a consumer inventory in the file header; a third consumer needs the other two's coefficients re-read in the same edit.
- **Wait one passage before acting on a hue-share number** (iter21→22). hueMix.red swung 0.09 → 0.23 → 0.06 across three ticks with no edit; the selector tightening that looked warranted at 0.23 would have sharpened the flip for nothing. A share meter needs two passages of the same character before it is a defect.
- **A palette lock with a hard selector turns any fast hue modulation into a binary flip** (iter21). The iter19 flux twinkle was a legal 0.10 hue shimmer under a continuous palette; after the 0.44–0.56 selector it became orange↔purple strobing on every transient. After locking a palette, audit every audio term on the hue coordinate for RATE — only slow drivers (springs, arc, depth) may touch a thresholded hue.
- **A shader constant is not persistent state** (iter20c). `T0` baked into the .frag reset the whole hour arc on a reload. Anything the set depends on across time belongs in a URL param or a controller (like navZoom, which lattice-nav re-read from `&navZoom=`), never in a literal.
- **Detect reloads by `performance.now()`**, not by eye (iter20c): a 55 s page age with a 7-entry console was the tell; the screenshot only showed 'fat lines'.
- **High-chroma orange/yellow clips to RED in sRGB** (iter20b). OKLCH 68° at C 0.36 came out red (hueMix red 0.41, orange 0.00). Keep orange-side chroma ≤ ~0.22 at L 0.6–0.75, or scale chroma per anchor; purple survives clipping, orange does not.
- **Luma meters under-read red/purple palettes ~2×** (iter20b). lum/lumMax are Rec.601-weighted; a saturated purple line AT the L cap reads lumMax ~0.28. Once the palette is red/purple, brightness is judged by the OKLCH L cap and `whiteish`, never by lumMax — otherwise 'still dim' pushes drive until the lines go white.
- **A cap is not a drive** (iter20). Raising an L ceiling does nothing if the gain/exponent below it never lets `lit` reach it — lines stalled at L~0.28 under a 0.73 cap. Tune drive (gain, exponent) to REACH the cap, then let the cap do the white-protection; check lumMax against the cap value every tick the arc moves.
- **A named palette is a lush()-level lock** (iter20b). Map the existing hue coordinate onto anchor hues (`mix(h1,h2,smoothstep(fract(s)))`) so every hue driver keeps working and merely selects between the named colours; meter it with a hue-bucket share (`hueMix`).
- **The arc scalar is also the live intensity knob** (iter20). When the user wants more/less NOW, nudge T0 (jump the arc), don't hunt coefficients — every intensity term moves coherently and the build's endpoint is preserved. Pair the jump with a chroma push so the new light is colour.
- **An L cap only caps what goes through the ramp** (iter20/C10). Grep every `vec3(1.0)` / white `mix` after the cap lands — the tendril filaments bypassed it. Companion to HANDOFF §0.3's grep-every-`col +=`.
- **A spread number without an arc value is uninterpretable now** (iter19). lumSpread collapsed 0.163→0.04 purely because intensity went to ~0; every spread reading from here must be logged with gArc, and compared against readings at a similar arc, not against the pre-arc night.
- **Set arc.** When the user wants a build "over the course of an hour", make ONE monotonic arc scalar (`gArc = clamp((iTime-T0)/3600)`) and hang the intensity coefficients off it with `mix(now, later, gArc)` — never hand-brighten tick by tick (iter18). Every later tick tunes a shape, not a level; and the arc reads back as a number next to lum.
- **"Too white" on a projector is an L-cap problem, not a hue problem** (iter18). Lines at OKLCH L~0.86 read white on fabric whatever their hue; cap L (≤~0.5 for outlines) and push chroma. Add a `whiteish` meter (lum>0.35 AND sat<0.25) so the complaint has a number.
- **Read 6 s of features before choosing; the track profile, not the shader's wiring gaps, should pick the lane** (iter17 — first move chosen FROM a music read rather than from the wiring list). A 16-sample window (treble/centroid/entropy/roughness/crest + kick count + springs) names the dominant domain; one move from that lane, and write the passage profile next to the numbers so they have a context. Caveat measured the same tick: the track changed character within 2 min of the read (roughness 0.71→0.30–0.48), so a smoothed feature with a floor beats a hard-coded profile.
- A `#define` placed inside a function only renames text AFTER it. Never use #define to retarget a uniform for helper functions — use a global set at the top of mainImage. (Cost tonight: two 'proven' reactive moves were silently dead for 12 ticks; the meters attributed their motion to other terms.)
- Iris rule adopted: gate GEOMETRY hard, gate COLOUR softly with a floor. Iris rule adopted: audio in AMPLITUDE, never in PHASE/ANGLE.
- **Never trust one frame; measure a spread.** A screenshot and a single meter sample are both
  point samples of a moving signal, and their disagreement is *information about motion*, not about
  either instrument. Any "flashy" complaint should be answered with an N-frame spread before a single
  coefficient moves. Candidate: fold `lumSpread` into `aesthetic-meter.js` as a first-class metric
  next to `flicker`, which measures motion *jitter* and missed this because the pulse was smooth.
- **A mask has coverage, not just presence.** §0.3's `within` was a real mask that happened to cover
  the whole screen. Every `+=` layer deserves the question "where on screen does this multiplier
  reach zero" — if the answer is nowhere, it's a floor.
- **Weight-is-alpha accumulators need the SDF width as the subtlety knob.** When one scalar feeds
  both weight and alpha, "make it subtler" and "make it transparent" are the same edit. Reach for
  `gBorder`/`bw`, never the weight.
- **Audio → hue is the safest reactive channel this shader has.** The wave pulse reads as musical in
  hue and as a strobe in lightness. Where the next reactive term wants to go, try hue first.
- **A gate readout on the pad** would have made §0.2 a five-second diagnosis instead of an hour.
