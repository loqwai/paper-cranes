What goes on the tag

Bulk flashing: stay on one shape and tap every bead of that shape. The counter climbs and each write vibrates. Then press next shape. Counts are remembered on this phone.

Verify a bead by tapping it from the home screen: the popup must open the visual and the crest must be nameable on screen. Tags stay rewritable; nothing here locks them.

Short links: by default a bead carries 2cb.pw/mon-<shape>, which redirects to the visual. To change what every bead opens later (another visual, or production after the merge) re-point the eleven slugs with node scripts/nfc/short-links.mjs --shader satellites --origin prod instead of re-flashing. The shortener answers 301, so a phone that already followed a link may keep the old target until its cache expires.

Offline note: the tag stores only a URL. With no signal at the site the phone needs the visual already cached (open the same URL once beforehand so the PWA caches it) or a hotspot.
